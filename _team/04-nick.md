Nick is a certified teacher and a private educator from Chicago that currently works at The American School Foundation, in Mexico City. Having received a B.S. alongside a M.Ed. in Education, Nick brings a wealth of experience that comes from professional training during his 10 year career as a teacher, alongside 15 years of private education experience as a tutor and consultant. Using a combination of research-based strategies and teaching methods built throughout his career, Nick offers a personalized approach specific to each student and family. Nick has worked with students from elementary school to high school, in a variety of subject matters, and he prides himself in his adaptability that lets him cater services to best help each learner.

Nick strives to improve student's confidence, comfortability, and success working in mathematics. Mathematics is unique because a student's self-perception strongly influences their relationship in learning throughout their time in school. He works to improve students' image of themselves as math learners, while simultaneously building academic skills and relevant executive functioning skills. Nick uses a combination of teaching strategies, alongside Orton-Gillingham techniques, to provide personalized mathematics support in a comfortable, safe space for students - a place where it is fun to do math!
