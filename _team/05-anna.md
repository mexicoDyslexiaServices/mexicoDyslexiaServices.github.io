Anna is a Speech-Language Pathologist specializing in cognitive-linguistic rehabilitation and executive function coaching. With a background in neuroscience, she has always been fascinated by the unique ways the brain supports learning, communication, and daily life. Her experience in medical settings has allowed her to blend science and creativity, partnering with patients and families to develop individualized solutions to complex cognitive challenges. Her expertise in executive function assessment and intervention has led her to facilitate workshops, develop executive function group curricula, and present to school professionals on supporting students with cognitive and executive function needs. Outside of work, she enjoys reading, hiking, and going to the movies—popcorn is a must.

Research consistently shows that cognitive rehabilitation and executive function intervention are most effective when therapy is personalized, meaningful, and embedded in everyday life. Anna's practice combines patient-centered care with evidence-based approaches to address the real-world challenges that children and families face. She integrates principles of motor learning, metacognition, and family coaching into every treatment plan, empowering children to develop effective strategies that generalize beyond the therapy room and support lasting independence.
